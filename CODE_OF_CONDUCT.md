# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## English

Participants must communicate professionally, focus criticism on technical work, respect different experience levels, and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam, or disclosure of private server/player information. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

## 中文

参与者应保持专业沟通，将批评集中于技术工作，尊重不同经验水平。禁止骚扰、威胁、歧视、人肉搜索、垃圾信息，以及泄露服务器或玩家隐私。为保护项目社区，维护者可在必要时编辑或删除内容，并限制违规者参与。
