# WorldAreaReset 1.14.7

## English

- Use the configured restore block budget consistently when players are online; only chunks with nearby protected player blocks take the protected path.
- Remove the extra one-tick gap between chunks and increase online restore concurrency to four workers.

## 中文

- 玩家在线时也统一使用配置的恢复方块批次，只有存在附近玩家保护方块的区块才使用保护路径。
- 移除区块之间额外的一 tick 间隔，并将在线恢复并发数提升为四个工作单元。
