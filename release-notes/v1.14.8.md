# WorldAreaReset 1.14.8

## English

- Console-facing command responses, help lines, updater notices, and cleanup/restoration broadcasts now use the startup-banner palette instead of player-facing gradients.
- Console notification titles, labels/separators, values, and notices are rendered with distinct banner colors while player messages retain their configured styling.

## 中文

- 后台指令响应、帮助行、更新器提示以及清理/热恢复广播现在使用启动横幅配色，不再使用玩家端渐变。
- 后台通知按横幅颜色区分标题、标签/分隔线、值和提示，玩家消息继续保留配置中的样式。
